**Status.** No articles were written or published in this pass. These are **editorial topics, not verified facts**. Each needs DAMA's own knowledge (recipes, SOPs) and credible sources before publishing. Write them in Admin → Catatan Dapur, which has fields for the short answer, sources and related products.

## Article rules

1. **Answer first.** The title is the question, and the "Jawaban singkat" field answers it in 1–3 sentences.
2. **Structure.** H2 per sub-question; one idea per paragraph; links to the related product and line.
3. **Facts.** Cultural or historical claims need a source in "Sumber & rujukan". Product instructions (storage, reheating, ingredients) must match DAMA's SOP or label. If an SOP is missing, don't publish the instruction.
4. **Voice.** Hangat, tulus, jujur. No superlatives ("paling", "terenak", "nomor satu"), no fake urgency.
5. **Every article needs:** title, slug, category, short answer, body, author, featured photo with alt text, related products, and an SEO title and description. The modified date is automatic.
